# Activity 1b - Ryoji Ikeda Inspired Glitch Art

## Sketch Concept

This sketch creates a Ryoji Ikeda-inspired audiovisual composition with random white vertical lines, colorful circles, and cross patterns.

## Sketch Instructions

- **Mouse Click**: Creates white horizontal and vertical crosses at mouse position
- **Any Key Press**: Saves the current canvas as "activity1b-image.jpg"
- The sketch continuously generates random vertical lines and occasional colored circles
- Background fades over time creating motion trails
